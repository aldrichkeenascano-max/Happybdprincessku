My Message For You 💗

Happy Birthday Love,

Today is a celebration of you—the incredible person who brings so much light, kindness, and warmth into the lives of those around you. More than anything, I hope this year brings you peace, genuine happiness, good health, and every opportunity you've been dreaming of. You deserve every beautiful thing life has to offer.

Thank you for every memory we've shared, every laugh, every conversation, and every moment you've allowed me to be part of your life. Loving you has been one of the most meaningful experiences I've ever had, and I will always be grateful for that.

There is also something I want to say from the bottom of my heart.

I know I've hurt you. I know that my mistakes, my shortcomings, and the choices I made caused you pain that you never deserved. I can never change what happened, and I know that saying "I'm sorry" will never be enough to erase the hurt I caused.

If I could ask for one thing on your birthday, it would be this: I hope that, in time, you'll find it in your heart to forgive me. Not because I deserve it, but because I truly regret everything I put you through.

I'm deeply sorry for breaking your trust and for becoming the reason behind your tears. You deserved honesty, loyalty, and love, and I failed to give you those when it mattered most.

I know that healing doesn't happen because of beautiful words. It happens through honesty, consistency, and time. I don't expect this letter to erase the pain I caused, but I hope it shows you that I finally understand the weight of my mistakes and the value of the trust I once took for granted.

Thank you for giving me the chance to learn from my mistakes and become a better man. I know that trust is earned through actions, not promises, and I want to spend every day proving that I can be someone worthy of the love you've continued to show me.

On your special day, my wish isn't about us—it's about you. I hope you continue to grow, achieve your dreams, find happiness in every little moment, and always remember how valuable you are.

You deserve a life filled with peace, love, and countless reasons to smile.

Happy Birthday, my love.

No matter what tomorrow brings, I will always be thankful that I met you. Loving you has changed me, and I will never stop appreciating the wonderful person that you are.

Happy Birthday, and I love you—always.

— Aldrich
